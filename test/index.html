Refine faces (post-generation)

Re-draws small faces in a video you already generated: each person's face is tracked and cropped (ComfyUI-H3-FaceRefine's tracker), the crops are redrawn by Wan 2.2's low-noise model, and pasted back. Faces 120px tall and up are left untouched; full strength at 30px and below. Runs on the GPU session above. The first refine on a volume downloads the Wan models (~22.5GB, once). Upscaled sources are refined at native size and upscaled back.
